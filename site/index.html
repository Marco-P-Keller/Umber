Umber

Umber listens to your room for 15 seconds, works out what gets through – traffic, voices, snoring or a hum – and plays a noise shaped to cover exactly that. Generated live on your iPhone or iPad.

What it does

  • Room scan: measures the noise in your room in eight frequency bands and shapes a masking noise to match. Analysed on your device; nothing is recorded, saved or sent.
  • A saved noise for each room – bedroom, office, hotel.
  • Nine more sounds to blend in: brown, pink, white and green noise, fan, rain, ocean, wind and womb, each with its own level.
  • Sleep timer that fades out slowly over the last 20 seconds.
  • Plays with the screen off; works with Siri, Shortcuts and the Lock Screen.
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Works offline.
  • Available in 34 languages.

Umber needs no network connection. It asks for the microphone only when you start a room scan.

Umber

Brown noise, pink noise, white noise and six more calming sounds, generated live on your iPhone or iPad. Nothing loops, nothing repeats.

What it does

  • Nine sounds: brown, pink, white and green noise, fan, rain, ocean, wind and womb.
  • Blend them and set the level of each one.
  • Sleep timer that fades out slowly over the last 20 seconds.
  • Plays with the screen off; works with Siri, Shortcuts and the Lock Screen.
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Works offline.
  • Available in 34 languages.

Umber needs no permissions and no network connection.

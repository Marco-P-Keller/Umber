Support

Something not working, or an idea for a sound? Open an issue and describe your device, iOS version and what happened.

Common questions

The sound stops when I lock the phone.

It should keep playing. If it does not, check that another app has not taken over audio (a call, a video or a podcast), then start Umber again.

Is there really no loop?

Right. Every sound is computed sample by sample while you listen, so there are no recordings and no loop point.

How do I start a sound with Siri?

Say “Play brown noise in Umber” or “Stop Umber”. The same actions appear in the Shortcuts app.

What does Umber cost?

Nothing to download, and there is nothing to buy inside. No ads, ever.

Privacy

Last updated 29 September 2026

Umber collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. It never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your last sound mix, the “mix with other audio” setting and a count of listening sessions (used only to decide when to ask once for an App Store rating) are stored locally on your device. They are never sent anywhere and are removed when you delete the app.

Permissions

Umber asks for none: no microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos or notifications. Audio playback continues in the background so a sleep sound does not stop when the screen turns off.

Children

Umber contains no advertising, no links to purchases and collects nothing, so it is suitable for all ages.

Contact
